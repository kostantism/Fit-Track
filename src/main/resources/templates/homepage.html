🌐 Fit Track

The Smart Way to Manage Fitness & Training

The Fit Track web application modernizes fitness tracking and personal training management. Users can easily register, manage their profiles, and track their fitness-related activity through a clean and intuitive interface. Trainers gain access to structured tools that help them monitor users, organize training data, and support progress efficiently.

With secure authentication, role-based access, and a scalable architecture, Fit Track brings clarity, reliability, and structure to modern fitness management. A simple, secure, and flexible platform designed to support healthy lifestyles and effective training.
